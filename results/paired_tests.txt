READING IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The signed-rank test is significant while the sign test is not: the effect lives in
the MAGNITUDE of the differences, not merely their direction. 14/20 by itself would
not be convincing; 14/20 with large wins and small losses is.

Zero-shot does NOT beat same-session calibration - it loses 20/20. That gap is what
more wearers would have to close, and it is the honest limit of the claim.
